# Supporting MMEF and X6 models

M08 implements the supporting MMEF and X6 analyses. Its selected fits are three MMEF joint models, four X6 joint models, two paired-change GEE models, four repeated X6 models and two X6 trajectory analyses with full and reduced fits. All use verified M03 contracts. Spirometry trajectories are fitted separately in M06.

## MMEF joint models

The available-case pre-bronchodilator MMEF model supplies the retained prose estimates. Separate pre- and post-bronchodilator models use the common paired MMEF sample for Table S2. The available-case sample must not be replaced by the paired sample. A paired model's post-TB contrast is a lung-function difference between exposure states, not a within-visit bronchodilator response.

Each joint model includes the oriented resistance, compliance, end-expiratory reactance and end-expiratory resistance processes, an independent LCI process and MMEF. Process-specific nuisance regressions use visit category, standardized height and sex, trained on pre/no-TB observations. Each response is centered using its fitted nuisance regression and divided by the training residual SD, with degrees of freedom equal to the nuisance design column count. Post-diagnosis measurements after first TB at age five or later are excluded. Records before those diagnoses remain eligible.

The mechanics child factor has a unit-normal prior. Its indicator loadings and residual scales have half-normal priors with SD one. The separate LCI process is a unit-normal child effect multiplied by a half-normal scale. LCI contributes neither to the target mean nor to the TB hazard. The MMEF mechanics loading is normal with SD one; direct effects, process shifts, infancy additions and hazard associations are normal with SD 0.75. Residual scales have half-normal priors with SD one. The target mean also includes `target_process = tau_target * target_raw`, with a unit-normal raw child effect and `tau_target ~ HalfNormal(1)`. This target intercept is independent of mechanics in its prior and does not enter the other processes or the hazard.

First-TB events and censoring use the strict before-five definition, with piecewise hazard intervals 0–<1, 1–<3 and 3–<5 years. Follow-up stops at first eligible TB, study exit, the administrative endpoint or age five, whichever is earliest. Diagnosis on study exit or the endpoint counts; diagnosis exactly at five is outside the early event definition. No risk time after observed exit is credited. Lung-process measurements are separately restricted to study exit. Baseline log hazards have normal priors centered on the logarithms of 0.045, 0.018 and 0.006, with SD 1.25. The hazard includes the jointly estimated mechanics factor and sex.

For each draw, the age-one-to-under-five total effect is the direct effect plus target loading times mechanics shift. The infancy total adds both the direct infancy term and the loading times the infancy mechanics shift. The pooled before-five effect weights those totals by distinct post-TB target children in the two diagnosis-age groups. Native L/s estimates multiply the complete draw-wise contrast by the target residual scale. Six effect/component rows and two exponentiated hazard-association rows are retained per MMEF fit. Higher mechanics-factor values mean better mechanics, so its hazard label is "Higher (better) jointly estimated mechanics factor". The factor uses information from the complete joint fit, including later measurements; it is not a purely prospective pre-TB predictor. Hazard probabilities compare the exponentiated ratio with one.

## Paired MMEF change

The response is post-minus-pre MMEF within the same child/test-date row. Both original values are converted separately to double precision before subtraction, preserving the selected calculation. The prepared response's storage-precision reconciliation is audited; earlier preparation artifacts remain unchanged.

The change sample retains future-TB and post-later-TB states as separate covariates and therefore differs from the paired joint-model sample. The before-five and infancy designs are fitted separately, adjusting for visit, height and sex. The infancy design also retains the age-one-to-under-five state separately.

Gaussian independence GEE supplies the coefficient and child-cluster robust covariance, without an additional small-sample covariance multiplier. Coefficients are independently checked against ordinary least squares. The selected intervals use the robust SE and multiplier 1.96. Standardized reference and exposed changes average the observed covariate design after setting other TB states to zero. Whole-child bootstrap intervals use 2,000 resamples stratified by first-TB age group with seed 20260903. Exact leave-one-child-out coefficient ranges are separate influence summaries. Neither supporting interval replaces the selected robust interval. Only aggregate summaries are exported.

## Joint X6

Native X6 and South African reference X6 z-scores each have separate before-one and before-five fits. These are threshold-specific models; their before-five contrast is not a child-weighted combination of separate infancy and older-child effects. Target measurements require exact age three through seventeen years inclusive, valid dates, height and sex. Post-TB measurements following first TB at or after the selected cutoff are excluded.

The joint X6 model uses the four mechanics indicators and a unit-normal child factor, without an LCI process. Nuisance regressions again train on pre/no-TB observations. Their residual scale is the square root of training residual sum of squares divided by residual degrees of freedom. Indicator loadings and residual scales have half-normal priors with SD one; the target loading is normal with SD one. A separate target child intercept has the same unit-normal raw effect and HalfNormal(1) scale as the MMEF target and has no hazard coupling. The mechanics shift, direct target effect and hazard associations have normal priors with SD 0.75. Eligible hazard intervals are drawn from 0–<1, 1–<3 and 3–<5 years; baseline log hazards are normal with mean log(0.03) and SD 1.25. Sex and the jointly estimated mechanics factor enter the hazard. Follow-up ends at eligible first TB, observed exit, the administrative endpoint or the selected first/fifth birthday. Events must precede that birthday strictly; exit and administrative boundaries are inclusive. Measurements remain separately bounded by study exit.

Total X6 effect equals the direct effect plus target loading times mechanics shift. Total, direct and shared-mechanics effects are rescaled draw by draw to native hPa*s/L or reference SD. The selected source contains no separate X6 hazard result rows.

For all seven joint fits, posterior intervals condition on the fitted nuisance regressions and residual scales from preparation. Their uncertainty is not propagated into the second-stage model. Its omission need not change every interval in the same direction. The independent target intercept repairs a covariance restriction; it is not a universal interval multiplier or evidence of causal mediation through mechanics.

## Repeated X6 and trajectories

Repeated native and reference-z X6 models retain all eligible exact-age three-through-seventeen observations, with target-TB, later-TB and future-TB states separate. The model has a child random intercept and adjusts for visit, height and sex.

The reference-z trajectory analyses use the distinct exact-age three-through-ten sample. A natural cubic spline with three degrees of freedom replaces visit category. The full model interacts the target-TB state with all three spline terms; the reduced model estimates a parallel trajectory difference. Full and reduced models adjust for height, sex, later TB and future TB. Both reuse the unchanged general Gaussian REML engine from M06, including its selected variance search and child-cluster robust covariance correction `G/(G-1) * (N-1)/(N-P)`.

Predictions reuse the training knots and centering constraints. Sex is standardized to its measurement-weighted mean, height to its fitted cubic age relationship, and future/later TB states to zero. The age polynomial remains centered at 7.5 years. Each analysis produces 202 mean predictions on a 101-point grid and eight annual exposed-minus-reference contrasts. Full coefficient vectors, model and robust covariance matrices, variance components and sample counts accompany the outputs.

Curve intervals are pointwise 95% intervals for population means, not simultaneous bands or individual prediction intervals. They condition on the fitted height-standardization relationship and observed sex proportion. The common height/sex terms cancel exactly in the exposed-minus-reference prediction contrast; that cancellation does not supply a simultaneous-band guarantee. Normal-Wald and chi-square reference distributions remain asymptotic despite the finite-cluster covariance multiplier.

Selected inference includes the raw three-degree-of-freedom interaction Wald test, the overall trajectory test and reduced-model contrast. No multiple-testing adjustment is recomputed on the selected cutoff subset: the original exploratory adjustment families also contained an unselected cutoff.

## Execution and validation

```sh
.venv/bin/python run.py --through M08 --config config/paths.local.json --analysis config/analysis.local.json --resume
```

M08 verifies M01–M07 before fitting its selected models. `--supporting-sampling FILE` controls M08 only; `--sampling FILE` controls M04 and `--mechanics-sampling FILE` controls M07. The common M08 defaults use four chains, 3,200 retained draws, 2,200 tuning iterations, target acceptance 0.95 and maximum tree depth 10. The recorded fresh seed is the applicable source-wrapper seed plus the configured offset. Historical arrays establish retained chain/draw counts; archived audits do not establish all realized historical sampling controls. Wrapper defaults are identified separately from realized evidence.

MMEF uses `adapt_diag` with four distinct initial mechanics and target loadings (0.25, 0.35, 0.45 and 0.55) and initial target residual SD 0.9, following the established M04 initialization. The target loading remains an unconstrained normal parameter. This initialization was adopted after a jittered paired-post fit produced a persistent collapsed mechanics-factor chain with markedly lower log density. The initial failed run is retained privately; no TB contrast estimate selected the remedy. X6 retains `jitter+adapt_diag`.

Complete posterior variables, including child effects, are retained privately. Acceptance requires maximum rank-normalized R-hat at most 1.01, bulk and tail effective sample sizes at least 400, BFMI at least 0.3 in every chain, and no divergences or maximum-tree-depth hits. The corrective protocol additionally requires the observed pre/no-TB target ICC and within-child mean square to lie inside central 99% intervals from 2,000 conditional replicas using actual child membership and repeats. Eligibility requires at least 20 repeat children and 30 within-child degrees of freedom; an unevaluable screen blocks completion. See [the fixed adequacy protocol](CORRECTIVE_REVISION.md). Raw-fit and aggregate-validation fingerprints are separate. Resume verifies inventories, hashes, sampling controls and complete posterior shapes, then recomputes posterior aggregates, diagnostics and the covariance screen. Changed reporting code can validate unchanged raw draws without refitting.

The prior source-reproduction protocol selected the converged unpaired MMEF archive corresponding to the manuscript, separately from a similarly named unsuccessful archived fit. It compared posterior means and both interval endpoints for 36 selected effect/hazard rows, using four combined Monte Carlo standard errors, including endpoint-specific quantile MCSE. Its first fully converged batch used 1,600 retained draws per chain; one component upper-endpoint comparison exceeded the fixed limit. A documented common increase to 3,200 draws retained the same seeds, tuning, initialization and gates, with exact posterior and non-timing sampler prefixes. All 108 historical comparisons passed. That amendment and its original failed comparison remain private evidence of source reproduction. They are not acceptance tests for the revised target likelihood and corrected threshold censoring; old-versus-new estimates are compared descriptively, without forcing the new values to reproduce the old model.

Deterministic execution retains all ten component fits and eight aggregate tables. Resume recomputes selected fits and checks saved tables against those results. Failed diagnostics, inference or artifact checks cannot create a successful cumulative completion marker. Raw joint draws remain under private `supporting_fits/`; joint aggregates are under `supporting_joint/`, and deterministic aggregates under `supporting_repeated/`. Only a successful cumulative run writes `supporting_current.json`. Manuscript table and figure rendering remain in M13–M14; source-only GitHub delivery remains in M15.

The historical M08 synthetic suite had 437 passing tests, including 92 M08 additions. Its five-role review checked provenance, likelihoods, posterior and deterministic calculations, manuscript definitions, and failure/resume behavior, including all 117 deterministic coefficients and 2,782 covariance cells. Revised-model sampling, covariance adequacy, scientific comparisons and full reconstruction require their own source-bound receipts; the historical tests and fit acceptance are not claims that the corrective revision has passed.
